The shop

A quiet chair in a busy neighborhood

Sanctuary is Bam Cuts’ barbershop in the Strip District, a professional shop built around clean fades, sharp lines and unhurried service.

Every classic cut finishes with a blow dry and style, a hot lather neck shave and a lavender-infused hot towel. Kids are welcome in the chair, and there’s a restroom on site for clients.
